The Community Constitution

Article 1: Core Foundations

In this Community, we must uphold these crucial promises to ensure stability:

  1. We are united as one people1, regardless of race, ethnicity, culture, beliefs, heritage, nationality, and skin color.
  2. An act of bullying, harassment, racial discrimination, violence or disrespect that is directed at one of our members is considered a direct attack2 on our entire community; we stand united to protect, support, and defend one another while using reasonable measures and in accordance with school rules.
  3. We are committed to supporting3 our peers, especially new students, in academic activities, fostering strong bonds among us, and supporting them in every way possible.
  4. We must be responsible4 in all of our actions, ensure the highest level of honesty, and share crucial information with everyone to ensure fairness while following school institution laws.

Article 2: Rights for Members

As this community have multiple groups (School Info , 2nd Family, and C.O.G.11), there are rights:

  1. Any students who are in Grade 11, whether they are new or old, always have the right to join and leave5 the community/groups with no difficulty or reprecussions.
  2. Every member, whether new or old, has the absolute right6 to freely share their opinions, ideas, and present feedback in group discussions or conversations without facing fear of judgement, discrimination, or exclusion if the speech does not violate UAE cyber laws.
  3. Members, whether new or old, have the right7 to access and request academic assistance, homework reminders, and study materials without experiencing gatekeeping or resource hoarding from peers.
  4. Every member, whether new or old, has the right to personal privacy (subject to UAE & School Law). This includes private messages8 and personal details, and discreet messages may not be sent in public group chats.
  5. Every member, whether new or old, has the right9 to have access to School Info no matter what violations he committed unless he voluntarily leaves.
  6. Every member, whether new or old, has the right10 to vote on matters such as football polls, potential hangouts, peer-to-peer sessions, and structural changes that may occur occasionally on School Info and other platforms.

Article 3: Admin Rights & Responsibility for the Community

In this Community, admins must follow these rules and acknoleadge their rights and obligations:

  1. Admins must11 share academic resources, quiz dates, homework deadlines, and any information related to our school without intentionally withholding critical information. (e.g., not sharing exam resources with others who require them.)
  2. Admins must12 support their peers academically to the best of their abilities. without causing inconvenience for the admins (e.g., a personal session for a long period of time during exam season)
  3. Admins must13 maintain a professional, non-biased tone when sending announcements, helping their peers, and sending important reminders & notes without causing confusion or misinformation.
  4. Admins must14 respect the community’s rules (as they are not exempt from Articles 1 and 2), the school’s rules, and the government’s rules.
  5. Admins have the right to decline15 support to a fellow member if they are making excessive demands (such as a 2-hour study session personally or any aid that is extensive), or if that admin is busy with projects, exams, or personal matters.
  6. Admins have the right to temporarily16 appoint an admin from the members if if an active admin is absent, overwhelmed during exam periods, or unable to perform their duties.
  7. Admins have the right to step down17 (resign) from their position at any time with a 14-day deliberation grace period to reconsider before the resignation is formally finalized.

Article 4: Code of Conduct (Digitally & Phyisically)

To ensure stability, peace, and security. We must follow these guidlines :

  1. Members must18 resolve all forms of personal disagreements through private conversations (usually with an admin) or peer mediation before escalating to 2nd family or C.O.G.11.
  2. If a member within our community verbally or physically attacks another member (within the community), Article 1 will NOT be activated19; instead, other members must de-escalate the fight with these measures of diplomacy; if that fails, use reasonable force to break the fight or altercation, and report it to the nearest teachers or the school supervisor (If the incident occurred on the school premises).
  3. Members must20 avoid spamming stickers, pictures, videos, or messages in the community’s group.
  4. Members are prohibited21 from sending these messages/content to the groups, including: Adult content (inappropriate content in general), racial slurs, words that incite violence, discrimination, misleading videos, misinformation, gambling, drugs, private pictures (even if it is appropriate; violates their privacy), scam websites, grabbify-type of links, harsh racism, edited & altered pictures (includes the use of AI), unverified groups, sensitive political opinions, phrases that indicate bullying, leaked exam papers (in all fields; graded assignments, tasks, etc...), impersonation (on all forms), and all forms of digital privacy violations contents.

All members must know and recognize the admins of the community:

  1. All members must22 acknowledge that the following six admins, Ameer Alnaimi, Ibrahim Mehtab, Yamen Alawneh, Essam H., Fares Ibrahim and Unknown, are the legitimate admins of School Info and the entire Community.
  2. All members are recommended to23 respect the admins’ efforts in supporting the group (It’s not as easy as you think it is)
  3. All members must24 refrain from impersonating, intentionally undermining, or spreading rumours of a recognized admin.
